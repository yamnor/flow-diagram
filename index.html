Accepted formats and how to print them

Jordan–Wigner only. Each operation is U = exp(−iθH) for a Hermitian Pauli sum (real coefficients), or U = exp(θA) for an anti-Hermitian one (imaginary coefficients, e.g. jordan_wigner(T − T†)).

OpenFermion

for theta, gen in zip(thetas, generators):  # gen = T - T^dagger
    print(theta, '|', jordan_wigner(gen))

Qiskit (the label's rightmost letter is qubit 0)

for theta, op in zip(thetas, operators):    # SparsePauliOp
    print(theta, '|', op)

PennyLane — the gates of the ADAPT circuit as printed (no angle | needed)

for op in tape.operations:   # BasisState(...), DoubleExcitation(θ, wires=[...]), ...
    print(op)

or Pauli sums

for theta, op in zip(thetas, qubit_ops):
    print(theta, '|', op.simplify())
